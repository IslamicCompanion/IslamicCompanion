Features

What’s inside the app

Prayer Times & Azan

Today’s salah times and the next prayer, with your location or GPS, plus azan notifications.

Quran

Translation, Mushaf pages (604), audio and a Khatm option, all in one place.

Colour-coded Tajweed

Tajweed rules shown in colour in the Quran text, with a legend for Hamza, Madd, Qalqalah, Ikhfa, Iqlab, Ghunnah and more.

Hadith Collections

Read Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Jami at-Tirmidhi, Sunan Ibn Majah, Sunan Abu Dawud and more.

Dua & Zikr

Duas, adhkar, a tasbih counter and the 99 Names of Allah. Duas can be shared as an image.

Salah & Calendar

Prayer times, Qaza, Ramadan and Islamic events, with today’s Hijri date.

Learn

Noorani Qaida, tajweed rules, a New Muslim guide and an Islamic quiz to test your knowledge.

Islamic AI

Ask in any language. Offline search shows the original Quran and Hadith text with references, and online AI explains it using those sources.

Global Search

Search the Quran, Hadith, Duas, Asma and Surah from one place.

Offline Manager

Download content and manage settings so the essentials work without internet.

Bookmark & Share

Long-press an ayah for 2 seconds to bookmark it. Copy, share, add a note, make an image or open tafsir from the menu.

Hinglish Support

Switch to Hinglish from the top of the app to make reading easier.
